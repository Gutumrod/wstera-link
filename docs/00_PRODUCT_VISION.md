# WSTERA Link — Product Vision

**Status:** LOCKED pre-build baseline  
**Date:** 2026-08-26

## Product Definition
WSTERA Link is a branded campaign-link and click-analytics SaaS for Thai online sellers, creators, affiliate operators, agencies, and small businesses.

It is **not** positioned as a generic URL shortener. The product promise is:
> Create a stable branded link, know which channel sends customers, and change the destination without replacing published links or QR codes.

## Primary ICP
1. Thai online sellers using Facebook, LINE, TikTok, Shopee/Lazada affiliate links, or QR materials.
2. Creators/affiliate operators who need first-party outbound click measurement.
3. Small businesses that want branded links and simple campaign attribution without enterprise analytics complexity.
4. Agencies/social admins managing multiple campaigns for clients.

## Core Jobs To Be Done
- Publish a short/branded link that stays stable.
- Compare traffic sources by link/campaign.
- Change destination after a link/QR is already distributed.
- Track outbound affiliate clicks before leaving owned channels.
- Give a business owner enough evidence to decide where to spend effort.

## Product Principles
- Redirect reliability is more important than analytics completeness.
- Free must demonstrate value, not replace a paid plan indefinitely.
- Tenant isolation and entitlement checks are fail-closed.
- Analytics minimizes PII and does not promise unique visitors in V1.
- Paid value centers on custom domain, campaign analytics, higher limits, export/API, and team access.
- Module Hub is read-only upstream; WSTERA Link vendors copies and owns its adaptations.

## Non-Goals V1
- Full web analytics replacement.
- Ad-platform attribution or conversion pixel replacement.
- Link-in-bio builder.
- Marketing automation suite.
- A/B testing engine.
- Deep identity resolution or cross-device tracking.
- Enterprise SSO/SAML.
